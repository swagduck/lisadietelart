Oil & Mineral Practice

The Studio Practice

Crafted slowly with natural earth minerals, cold-pressed oils, and silence.

Working from a sunlit converted stone barn in the Hudson Valley, Aura Vance approaches every canvas as an unfolding dialogue between geological pigment and human recollection.

Each piece is constructed over weeks or months through translucent glazes, scraped impasto, and hand-ground lapis, raw umber, and chalk. The resulting surfaces gently transform throughout the day as sunlight shifts across the room.
